# Design assets

Images kept for later use. The app shows the drawn category icons in `public/categories/`, generated by
`scripts/category-icons.mjs`; the photo tiles below are kept in case we switch back.

- `category-photos/in-app/`: the photo tiles the app used for each category before switching to drawings.

- `category-photos/`: photo-style tiles not used yet, ready for categories we may add:
  - trade and home services: `ELECTRICIAN`, `PLUMBING`, `HVAC` (AC repair), `HANDYMAN`, `ROOFING`, `GUTTERS`,
    `PAVERS` (pavers and patios), `LANDSCAPING`, `PEST_CONTROL`, `REMODELING`
  - shopping, for splitting Clothing & Jewelry or Other Goods later: `MENS_WEAR`, `WOMENS_WEAR`, `KIDS_WEAR`,
    `FASHION_JEWELRY`, `ACCESSORIES`, `FABRICS`, `ELECTRONICS`, `HOME_APPLIANCES`, `KITCHENWARE`,
    `SPORTS_FITNESS`, `PET_SUPPLIES`, `PERSONAL_CARE`, `BABY_CARE`, `OFFICE_STATIONERY`, `GARDENING_TOOLS`,
    `BOOKS_MEDIA`
  - auto services: `CAR_DETAILING`, `CAR_BODY_SHOP`, `CAR_TIRES`, `OIL_CHANGE`, `CAR_DIAGNOSTICS`
  - event types, e.g. for the Events page filters: `EVENT_CONCERTS` (concerts and festivals), `EVENT_POOJAS`
    (poojas and religious), `EVENT_MELAS` (melas and fairs), `EVENT_WEDDINGS` (weddings and celebrations),
    `EVENT_CORPORATE`
  - `JOBS`, and `MUSIC` (music lessons; an alternative picture for Dance, Music & Sangeetham)
- `category-illustrations/`: the earlier illustrated tiles for rooms, tutoring, food, legal, domestic help,
  dance, realtors and events, replaced by photos.
- `source-sheets/`: the original sheets the tiles were cut from (5 tiles per row; cut to 172 px squares with rounded corners).

Photos used earlier: `HANDYMAN` for Home Services, `CAR_DETAILING` for Auto, `PERSONAL_CARE` for Beauty & Wellness,
`OFFICE_STATIONERY` for Tax & Accounting (copies in `in-app/`).

To use a photo instead of a drawing, copy it to `public/categories/` and point `CategoryIcon` at it.

## Subcategory icons

- `source-sheets/subcategory-icons-1.webp` to `-4.webp` (and `-3b.webp`, a redraw of sheet 3 whose better
  tiles replaced sheet 3's, including the mandap for Pujari weddings): the illustrated subcategory icons, cut to
  `public/subcategories/<CATEGORY>/<SUBCATEGORY>.png` (200 px, rounded) and listed in
  `src/lib/subcategory-art.ts`. Tiles were matched by their labels; sheet 3's fire tile (labelled Wedding) is
  Homam, and tiles that didn't fit their subcategory were left out. Sheet 4's tiles are padded onto the
  others' cream background so they sit the same size.
- The drawn `.svg` next to each one (from `scripts/subcategory-icons.mjs`) is the fallback for any new
  subcategory until it gets an illustration.
